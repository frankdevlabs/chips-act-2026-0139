# Commission extracts — working rules

> The base-text extract set for **COM(2026) 504 final** is transcribed: one file per `tracker.yaml`
> `extract_slices` (`COM-2026-504_<slice>.md`, 12 files — 11 operative slices + a curated recitals
> subset). Anchors are `article-N` / `recital-N`. Source quirks are preserved verbatim and flagged
> inline — headline ones: Art 1 numbers its paragraphs **1, 1, 2, 3** (duplicate "1."); Art 4
> continues Art 3's numbering (3–6); Art 30(1) cites "Directives **2024/23/EU**" (apparently
> 2014/23/EU); Art 42(8) cites "Article 55(6)" for comitology (Art 56 is the committee article);
> the Art 42/43 cross-references oscillate (Arts 14(6)(b)/18(9)/51(4)–(5) say 43(1) where Art 15(8)
> says 42(1)); Art 49(2) letters its points (f)–(n) with no (a)–(e); unresolved placeholders
> `[insert footnote]` (Art 2(26)) and `Regulation (EU) XXXX/XXXX [CADA] / [European Competitiveness
> Fund]` (Arts 7(3), 13(2)–(3)). The memorandum (not the operative text) misstates crisis-stage
> activation as a Commission implementing act — Art 39(3) gives it to the **Council by QMV**.
> Cross-check against the committed PDF under
> [`../../sources/commission/`](../../sources/commission/) before relying on any passage.

Before adding or editing any file in this directory, read
[`_TRANSCRIPTION_GUIDE.md`](./_TRANSCRIPTION_GUIDE.md) and follow it. It adapts the canonical
[`../council/_TRANSCRIPTION_GUIDE.md`](../council/_TRANSCRIPTION_GUIDE.md) for the **base text** —
the Commission proposal **COM(2026) 504 final** that future Council compromises will amend. These
extracts are the diff baseline for the `../council/` files.
