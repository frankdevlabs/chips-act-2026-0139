# Chips for Europe Initiative 2.0 and the demand-side instruments (Chapter II, Arts 3–12)

> **Baseline: Commission proposal COM(2026) 504.** Operative text:
> [extracts — Chapter II, Arts 3–12](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-504_chips-initiative-art3-12.md).
> No co-legislator position yet.

## What the proposal does

- **Arts 3–4** — continues the Chips for Europe Initiative as **Initiative 2.0**, funded under the
  current MFF by Horizon Europe and the Digital Europe Programme
  ([Art 3](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-504_chips-initiative-art3-12.md#article-3)). Now
  **six operational objectives**: (1) design capacities/virtual design platform; (2) pilot lines and
  pilot testbeds; (3) quantum chips; (4) competence-centre network; (5) **new — photonic integrated
  circuits**; (6) "Chips Fund" access-to-finance activities
  ([Art 4](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-504_chips-initiative-art3-12.md#article-4), detail in
  [Art 5](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-504_chips-initiative-art3-12.md#article-5)).
- **Art 6** — the European network of **competence centres** (selected by the Chips Joint
  Undertaking) continues, with skills/talent tasks.
- **Art 7** — **Grand Challenges**: large-scale cross-sectoral initiatives (next-generation AI
  chips, energy efficiency, heterogeneous integration…), coordinated with CADA's grand challenges
  and the EuroHPC JU —
  [Art 7](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-504_chips-initiative-art3-12.md#article-7).
- **Art 8** — **Demand Accelerators**: Commission + Member States stimulate uptake of EU-designed/
  manufactured chips in key markets (cloud, automotive, aeronautical, telecom, defence); off-taker
  requirement-setting, common technical roadmaps, co-design partnerships — implemented by the
  **Industrial Alliance for Semiconductors** —
  [Art 8](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-504_chips-initiative-art3-12.md#article-8).
- **Art 9** — a **Demand Forum** showcasing technologies of ESTIs, strategic projects and
  2023-Act-recognised facilities, also Alliance-run —
  [Art 9](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-504_chips-initiative-art3-12.md#article-9).
- **Art 10** — **chip innovation procurement**: the Chips JU supports cross-border joint procurement
  and first deployment of systems integrating semiconductors —
  [Art 10](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-504_chips-initiative-art3-12.md#article-10).
- **Arts 11–12** — synergies with Union programmes (Annex IV); implementation entrusted to the
  **Chips Joint Undertaking**; delegated acts may amend Annex I (actions) and Annex III (indicators).

## What changed vs the 2023 Act

Photonics added as a sixth component; a "strong focus on industrialisation and demand-stimulating
measures" (Grand Challenges, Demand Forum, Demand Accelerators) where the 2023 Act was
supply-driven; the never-used **European Chips Infrastructure Consortium (ECIC)** is dropped.

## Why it's contested

The demand instruments carry no purchase obligations and no new budget — whether they bite depends
on the Industrial Alliance and on procurement practice; the Chips Fund's predecessor budget was
exhausted in two years — see [`fault-lines.md`](../fault-lines.md).
