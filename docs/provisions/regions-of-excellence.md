# European Semiconductor Regions of Excellence (Chapter III s3, Arts 26–29)

> **Baseline: Commission proposal COM(2026) 504.** Operative text:
> [extracts — Ch III s3, Arts 26–29](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-504_regions-of-excellence-art26-29.md).
> No co-legislator position yet.

## What the proposal does

- **Art 26** — the Commission may award a **European Semiconductor Region of Excellence label** by
  implementing act, on application by a **regional authority**, explicitly endorsed by the Member
  State; the label "may be taken into account" when assessing operations under Union funding
  programmes —
  [Art 26](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-504_regions-of-excellence-art26-29.md#article-26).
- **Art 27** — the application's core is a **Semiconductor Region Investment Plan**: a ten-year
  strategic vision plus enabling conditions (clean energy and grid capacity, land/zoning, transport/
  water infrastructure, permitting simplification linked to the [one stop shop](permitting.md)),
  research and innovation links (pilot lines, competence centres), skills/workforce measures and
  investment-attraction measures incl. SME integration —
  [Art 27](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-504_regions-of-excellence-art26-29.md#article-27).
- **Art 28** — Commission assessment on **coherence and credibility** of the Plan and the level of
  commitment of the authorities; decision within three months of a complete application; label
  duration set by the decision; yearly progress reporting to the Commission and the ESB; a
  non-sensitive summary of the Plan is published to attract investment —
  [Art 28](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-504_regions-of-excellence-art26-29.md#article-28).
- **Art 29** — a **Network of European Semiconductor Regions of Excellence** (self-organising,
  rotating chair; best-practice exchange, cross-regional synergies, visibility for investment),
  with Commission facilitation —
  [Art 29](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-504_regions-of-excellence-art26-29.md#article-29).

## What it is (and is not)

A **signalling instrument**: per the memorandum, the label tells international investors a region
has a high-potential semiconductor ecosystem. It carries **no own budget and no automatic funding
preference** — only the soft "may be taken into account" link to Union programmes. New vs the 2023
Act (no regional layer existed). The Network feeds the ESB as a stakeholder organisation (Art 46(4)).

## Why to watch it

Low-conflict on substance, but a natural vehicle for cohesion-minded delegations and the CoR (a
mandatory consultee — [advisory](../advisory/README.md)) to seek harder funding links; watch whether
Council or EP tie the label to ERDF/ECF eligibility — see [`fault-lines.md`](../fault-lines.md).
