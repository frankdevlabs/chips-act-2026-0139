# Related instruments

Chips Act 2.0 **repeals one regulation and amends nothing else**: Article 58 of COM(2026) 504
repeals Regulation (EU) 2023/1781 (the 2023 European Chips Act), with transitional provisions in
Article 59 and a correlation table in Annex VII. Everything else on this page is a framework the
proposal **builds on or interfaces with** (verified against the proposal text). This page replaces
the per-amended-instrument pages a tracker normally carries; split out a page per instrument if a
compromise text ever introduces actual amendments.

| Instrument | CELEX / ref | Relationship to Chips Act 2.0 |
|---|---|---|
| **European Chips Act** | [2023/1781](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1781/oj) | **REPEALED** (Art 58; references read per the Annex VII correlation table). Transitional: 2023/1781 keeps applying to integrated production facilities and open EU foundries already recognised under its Art 15(3), for the duration of their status (Art 59). *Note: the SWD(2026) 504/505 cover pages misprint the repealed act as "2023/1782"; the proposal and Art 58 say 2023/1781.* |
| **Chips Joint Undertaking** | Council Reg [2021/2085](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2021/2085/oj) | Implementation backbone: Initiative 2.0's operational objectives 1–5 and grand challenges are entrusted to the Chips JU (Art 12), which also runs strategic-project calls (Art 18) and chip innovation procurement support (Art 10); "Chips Joint Undertaking" is defined to include any successor entity under the next MFF (Art 2(37)) |
| **Horizon Europe** | Reg [2021/695](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2021/695/oj) | Funding backbone 1: research and innovation activities of Initiative 2.0 under the current MFF (Art 3(2), Art 4(6)) |
| **Digital Europe Programme** | Reg [2021/694](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2021/694/oj) | Funding backbone 2: capacity-building activities of Initiative 2.0 (Art 3(2), Art 4(6)); the 2023 Chips Act had amended this regulation (Specific Objective 6) |
| **Public-procurement directives** | Dirs [2014/23/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/23/oj), [2014/24/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/24/oj), [2014/25/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/25/oj) | Define "contract"/"contracting authority"/"contracting entity" (Art 2(26)–(28)); scope the security-of-supply declarations (Art 30(1) — printed there as "Directives **2024/23/EU**, 2014/24/EU or 2014/25/EU", an apparent misprint); govern preventive joint procurement (Art 37(3): Arts 38–39 of 2014/24/EU, Arts 56–57 of 2014/25/EU) |
| **Financial Regulation** | Reg (EU, Euratom) [2024/2509](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2509/oj) | Crisis-stage common purchasing is carried out by the Commission under its own-procurement rules (Art 43(4), (7)); recital 55 invokes its Art 136 to restrict third-country participation in strategic projects |
| **NIS2 Directive** | Dir [2022/2555](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj) | Its Annex I (high-criticality) and Annex II (other critical) sectors scope the procurement security-of-supply regime (Art 30(1)) |
| **Export regime** | Reg [2015/479](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2015/479/oj) | The ESB assesses possible protective (export) measures against it in a crisis (Art 40(3)(b)) |
| **Defence-sector regulation** | Reg (EU) 2025/2643 | Chips Act 2.0 applies without prejudice to its defence-procurement procedures (Art 1(3)); recital 98 coordinates its Chapter VII security-of-supply / priority-rated-order regime with Arts 40–43 *(title not spelled out in the proposal — verify on first citation)* |

**Pending proposals the text leans on** (cross-referenced, not yet adopted): the **European
Competitiveness Fund** (COM(2025) 555 — support route for strategic projects, Art 13), the
**Regulation on speeding-up environmental assessments** (permitting interface, Art 23), the
**European Business Wallets** Regulation (single access portal, Art 24), and **CADA** itself
(grand-challenge coordination, Art 7(3) — file 2026/0138, tracked in the sibling repo). The
EuroHPC Joint Undertaking (Council Reg [2021/1173](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2021/1173/oj))
is a named coordination partner for grand challenges.
