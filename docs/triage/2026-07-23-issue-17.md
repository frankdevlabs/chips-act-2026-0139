## What was flagged

A new ESIA-hosted position dated **22 July 2026** — *"Innovate in Europe, scale in Europe: A joint
industry call for an ambitious European Competitiveness Fund"*. The tracker flagged it because the
European Competitiveness Fund (ECF) is the next-MFF vehicle expected to finance the **Chips for Europe
Initiative 2.0** under 2026/0139, so an industry call on the ECF bears on this file's financing.

Confirmed against the source PDF
([statement](https://www.eusemiconductors.eu/sites/default/files/2026.07.22_JointIndustryStatement_InnovateinEuropeScaleinEurope.pdf),
2 pp, retrieved 2026-07-23). Substance (three asks):
1. **Strengthen industry engagement on ECF priorities** — back a **Strategic Stakeholders Board** with
   effective industry participation; capture the whole value chain, cross-sectoral input.
2. **Closely link research and industrial-policy funding** — fully align ECF governance with **Horizon
   Europe** (integrated work programme, shared priorities), support the full innovation pathway to first
   industrial deployment and scale-up, and make **private-investment mobilisation ("crowd-in") a core
   success criterion**.
3. **Move at the speed of technology** — single rulebook + one-stop-shop across major programmes, faster
   time-to-grant (welcomes the next Horizon's Art 26 ≤7-month cap), simplified reporting.
Closes with a plea that **the ECF and Horizon Europe not be cut in the final MFF agreement**.
